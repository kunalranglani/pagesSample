Adding Fractions

24 easy questions with same and different denominators. Every answer is one whole or less.

Same bottom numbers? Add the top numbers and keep the bottom number: 1/5 + 2/5 = 3/5.
Different bottom numbers? Make them match first: 1/2 + 1/4 = 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4.
You do not need to simplify your answers.
